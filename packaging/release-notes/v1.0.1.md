SwiftSpot 1.0.1 plays on your Mac without asking you to pick a device first.

### New

- **The first song plays on this Mac.** With nothing playing anywhere, pressing play asks Spotify once to approve this Mac, then plays there. Before, it asked you to choose a device and left you to find This Mac under Play On. Free accounts, which can't play on the Mac, are still asked to choose a device.
