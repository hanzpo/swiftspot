SwiftSpot 1.1.0 fixes sign-in and first play getting stuck, and finds lyrics more often.

### Fixed

- **Sign-in.** When your sign-in expires, the app goes back to the sign-in screen instead of showing a library that won't load. Playback also connects at launch when Spotify answers slowly.
- **First play.** You can cancel the playback approval from This Mac under Play On, and pause cancels a song still waiting to start. A waiting song no longer plays later after you sign out.
- **Lyrics.** SwiftSpot asks Spotify for lyrics the way Spotify's own app does, so songs that showed none may have them now. A busy lyrics service is asked again before the app gives up.
- **Repeat.** A saved Repeat One no longer loops the first song you pick after launch, and repeat changes made on another device stick.
- **Resume.** A song its playlist no longer holds picks up where you left it, instead of starting the playlist's first song.
- **Liked Songs.** Playing a song there plays the whole list, not just the songs loaded so far.
- **Playing song.** It lights up only in the playlist it plays from.
- **Pause after this song** works with Repeat One.
- **Karaoke** recovers from a model error, and turning it off and on again reloads a model that failed.
- **Keyboard shortcuts** keep working when a song row has focus.

Several fixes come from [Spotifast](https://github.com/crmne/spotifast) by Carmine Paolino.
