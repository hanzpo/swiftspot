SwiftSpot 1.2.0 runs as one app instead of two, uses less memory, and stays idle when nothing is happening. It also adds Jam.

### New

- **Jam.** Start a Jam, invite people with its link, and see who is listening, from Playing Next. To join someone else's Jam, paste its link into Search.
- **One app.** The playback engine now runs inside SwiftSpot instead of as a separate helper app. On a signed-out test, memory use dropped by about 40%, and the app no longer wakes the CPU every few hundred milliseconds when nothing is playing. The download is about a third smaller.

### Fixed

- **Long playlists** load more smoothly: each new page of songs no longer makes the app reload every song before it.
- **Liking a song** no longer reloads the page you are on, and a like that fails no longer leaves the wrong heart showing.
- **Sign-in, Settings and Play On** update when something changes instead of checking every second or two.
- **Quick clicks.** Playing one song and then another, or skipping quickly, no longer lets an older answer replace the newer song, queue or lyrics.
- **Volume and shuffle** changes no longer redraw every song on the page.
- **Playlist covers.** Choosing a large picture no longer holds up the rest of the app while it is prepared.
- **Vocals slider** lets go after a lost drag, as the volume slider does.
- **Next up** leaves out the repeats Spotify adds to a short queue when Repeat is off (#1192).
- **Recent** shows a song once when Spotify also reports the same play (#1189).
- **The playing playlist** is remembered across a restart.

After updating, macOS may ask once to let SwiftSpot use its saved sign-in in your keychain. Choose Always Allow.

Several fixes come from [Spotifast](https://github.com/crmne/spotifast) by Carmine Paolino, with #1189 and #1192 by Espaye.
